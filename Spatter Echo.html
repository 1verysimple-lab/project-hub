Spatial Sniper

PROJECT ECHO

1. The Mission

A hidden sniper will fire 3 shots. You have 2.0 seconds to locate him using only sound.

2. Scoring Rules

  • 1st Hit (< 0.5s): 15 Pts + 12 Dmg (Perfect)
  • 1st Hit (> 0.5s): 10 Pts + 12 Dmg
  • 2nd Hit: 5 Pts + 8 Dmg
  • Miss: You take damage. He stays put.

3. Spatial Audio (HRTF)

This app uses Head-Related Transfer Functions. It filters sound frequencies to trick your brain into hearing depth (Front vs Rear) on stereo headphones.

*Headphones are mandatory.*
