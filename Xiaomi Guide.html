Enable Animated Wallpapers

  1. Open Settings → Wallpaper.
  2. Choose Create or AI Dynamic Wallpaper and select a photo.
  3. Tap Generate then Apply. Use on‑device mode for privacy and battery savings[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://www.mi.com/global/brand/ai/xiaomi-hyperai/?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "2").

Turn On Live Transcription and Meeting Notes

  1. Open Settings → Accessibility or HyperAI tools.
  2. Enable Live Transcription and grant microphone permission.
  3. Record a meeting from the recorder app; use the assistant to summarize or export notes.

Tip: enable on‑device NPU mode for long recordings to reduce battery drain[43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054](https://www.gsmarena.com/xiaomi_15t_pro-review-2888p4.php?citationMarker=43dcd9a7-70db-4a1f-b0ae-981daa162054 "3").

Use Multimodal Assistant Prompts

  1. Open the assistant (gesture or long‑press home) and tap the image icon.
  2. Attach a photo and type your prompt (e.g., “suggest captions” or “improve composition”).
