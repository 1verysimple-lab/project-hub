Centralis

Integrated B2B Restaurant Ordering Solution: RegTech-Enabled Predictive Procurement Platform

Executive Summary & Core Value Proposition

Centralis is designed to unify the fragmented B2B foodservice supply chain into a single, comprehensive digital ecosystem. Our competitive advantage is built upon solving the operational friction caused by mandatory compliance and financial risk.

Strategic Mission: Free Service Launch for SMEs (v1.0.0). The immediate strategic focus is to secure rapid adoption among small/local restaurants and flexible distributors by offering the core service for free. This strategy maximizes market traction, validates core utility, and generates the necessary data to attract institutional Seed investment for the high-cost, high-value regulatory features in Phase 2.

The Three Pillars of Value

  1. **RegTech Compliance Moat:** Native integration with complex government systems (e.g., Spanish *Factura Electrónica* and SII VAT reporting) automates mandatory legal processes, making the platform indispensable.[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span)
  2. **FinTech Risk Transfer Moat:** Integrated B2B Trade Credit (BNPL) ensures suppliers receive payment upon shipment, eliminating their credit risk, fraud protection, and debt collection burdens.[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)
  3. **Economic Moat (AI ROI):** Leveraging AI-powered predictive analytics to optimize ordering, reduce overstock, and deliver a quantifiable 5% reduction in food costs for restaurants.[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)

The Centralis Roadmap: App Development & Functionality

This sequential roadmap is designed to prioritize rapid delivery of core utility, ensuring interest and adoption by smaller, flexibility-focused providers first (Phase 1), before deploying the high-cost, high-value regulatory moats (Phase 2).

Phase 1: Minimum Viable Product (MVP) - Version v1.0.0 (Free Service / Pre-Seed Funded)

Primary Goal: Rapid Adoption & Workflow Simplification for SMEs. The focus is on replacing fragmented manual ordering (voicemail, email, text) with a simple, standardized, mobile-first digital exchange to achieve immediate time-saving utility for small providers. This phase requires zero ERP integration complexity.

Supplier Mobile App (SME Focus) Functionality

The supplier app is optimized for phone screens, enabling the provider to manage sales, pricing, logistics, and communications instantly, without needing a desktop system. This flexibility is key to securing early provider interest.

  • **Catalog & Pricing Management:** Allows the provider to maintain a **full product list** available to connected users, including **unit prices** and product availability status. Supports catalog creation via CSV upload or manual mobile entry.
  • **Flexible Pricing Control:** Implements foundational support for **volume-based tiered pricing** (quantity breaks) and the configuration of unique, **customer-specific negotiated rates** visible only to the contracted buyer.[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)
  • **Sales History and Performance:** Provides providers with a searchable **history of all sold products** (by customer, date, and quantity). Includes **Snapshot Analytics** (e.g., Top 3 Selling Items, Monthly Growth Trend) to enable early trend analysis and stock evaluation.
  • **Sales Rep Enablement:** Allows sales representatives to place orders on behalf of their assigned buyer accounts directly through the mobile app, leveraging the existing sales channel for rapid GMV growth.
  • **Order Logistics Management:** Allows the supplier to **set order cut-off deadlines** (e.g., "Must order by 3 PM for next-day delivery") and actively **update delivery time estimates** in a dedicated field, providing crucial transparency to the buyer.
  • **Integrated Communication Service:** Includes an **independent, in-app chat/messaging service** between the supplier and the buyer, eliminating the need to search through personal texts or emails for order-specific questions and streamlining communication.[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span)
  • **Simple Promotion Management:** Provides tools to create and push out time-limited promotions or "specials" directly to their connected list of users, replacing mass emails/texts and acting as a basic sales enablement tool.
  • **Order Inbox (The Hub):** Centralized notification and viewing of all new orders, replacing manual notes. Orders arrive as **standardized digital dockets** with simple, tap-to-change fulfillment status toggles.
  • **Low-Friction Synchronization:** Orders are transmitted via a basic mechanism (e.g., API/structured email export) for manual entry into the supplier’s internal system. Real-time ERP sync is explicitly deferred to Phase 2.
  • **Documentation Focus:** Prioritize creating simple **Quick Start Guides** to minimize staff training and resistance, maximizing adoption among small teams.

Documentation & Flexibility Strategy

A strategy focused on flexibility and open standards is essential to mitigate **vendor lock-in**—a material risk cited by enterprises adopting integrated solutions. This ensures the platform is viable for all sizes and future-proofed for expansion.

Key Documentation Mandates:

  • **API-First Architecture:** All core services will maintain comprehensive, versioned **API documentation** (v1.0.0, v1.5.0, etc.) to ensure seamless, modular integration with external systems, preventing proprietary lock-in.[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span)
  • **Data Standardization:** Prioritize the use of standard, open data formats (e.g., UBL/CII for e-invoices) and modern API protocols (JSON/XML) for synchronous, real-time data exchange, moving away from rigid, legacy EDI standards.[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span)
  • **Flexibility & Interoperability:** Ensure the platform supports free interconnection and interoperability between private e-invoicing platforms, as mandated by the *Crea y Crece* Law, guaranteeing users are not forced to use a single proprietary solution.[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span)
