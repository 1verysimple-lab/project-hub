🚀 Bianca's ADHD Referral Mission

Let's get this sorted. Follow these 3 steps.

1 The Form (Boring but Vital)

You need to fill out the ASRS Form. This is the "ticket" to get the referral. Be honest—answer based on your worst days, not your good ones.

📄 Download ASRS PDF

Download it, fill it out, and save it to your phone/computer.

2 The Message (Copy This)

Don't worry about writing an email. I've written it for you. It uses the correct legal words to stop them saying "no."

Subject: Right to Choose Referral Request - ADHD Assessment - Bianca Beaney Dear Referrals Team, I am writing to exercise my legal right to choose my mental healthcare provider under the NHS Constitution. I would like to be referred to **Psicon Ltd** for an Adult ADHD assessment and medication titration. **Why I am choosing this provider:** I understand that Psicon is a commissioned provider for the Kent & Medway ICB. As a local provider based in Canterbury, they offer the stability of face-to-face support if required and are aligned with local Shared Care protocols. **Provider Details for Referral:** • Provider: Psicon Ltd • NHS Email: kmicb.psicon.adults@nhs.net • Address: Delandale House, 37 Old Dover Road, Canterbury, CT1 3JF **My Request:** 1. Please complete the standard "Kent & Medway Adult RTC Referral Form". 2. Please attach my completed ASRS v1.1 Self-Report Form (attached to this message). 3. Could you please confirm that if Psicon diagnoses me and stabilises me on medication, the practice is willing to accept a Shared Care Agreement for ongoing prescriptions? Thank you, Bianca Beaney
