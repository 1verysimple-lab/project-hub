🎧 Mumble LAN: Pro Config

High-Fidelity • Low Latency • Positional Audio

1. Quick Setup Recap

If you haven't installed it yet:

  1. Install Mumble Client on both PCs.
  2. Install Murmur (Server) on YOUR PC only.
  3. Find your IP (cmd -> ipconfig).
  4. Connect both PCs to that IP.
Pro Tip: On LAN, connect using a wired Ethernet cable if possible. WiFi works, but Ethernet guarantees 0% packet loss for voice.

2. Unlock "Advanced" Mode

Mumble hides the good stuff by default. Let's unlock the full UI.

  1. Open Mumble.
  2. Click Configure in the top menu -> Settings.
  3. Crucial Step: Look at the bottom-left corner of the Settings window. Check the box that says Advanced.

You will see many new tabs appear on the left side. We will use these to fine-tune your experience.

3. Audio Quality (LAN Tuning)

Most voice apps compress audio to save data. On LAN, we don't care about data. Let's max it out.

Tab Audio Input
Compression / Quality 72 kb/s (or higher)
Audio per packet 10 ms (Lower latency)
Noise Suppression RNNoise (Best for gaming)

Why RNNoise? It uses AI to filter out your mechanical keyboard clicks (Cherry MX switches) while keeping your voice clear.

4. Refining "Voice Activity"

For a natural "couch co-op" feel, you don't want to press a button to talk.

  1. Go to Audio Input -> Transmission.
  2. Select Signal to Noise (This is smarter than simple Amplitude).
  3. Calibrate: Talk normally. The bar will move.
    • Move the slider so the bar is in the Green zone when you talk.
    • It should drop to the Red zone when you are just typing or breathing.
  4. Silence Below: Set this to roughly 500ms. This prevents the mic from cutting off instantly if you pause for a split second.

5. Positional Audio (The "Plugins")

This is the "Killer Feature" for CS and shooters. If she is to your left in the game, you will hear her voice from your left headphone ear cup.

  1. Go to Configure -> Settings -> Plugins.
  2. Click "Link to Game and Transmit Position".
  3. In the list, ensure the plugin for "Counter-Strike" (or Source Engine) is checked/enabled.
  4. Click Apply.

Note for CS2 / CS:GO:

Sometimes you need to enable the Audio Output -> Positional Audio checkbox as well. Ensure headphones are checked, not speakers.

6. The Overlay (HUD)

See who is talking without Alt-Tabbing. Useful if you sometimes forget if your mic is open.

  1. Go to Settings -> Overlay.
  2. Check Enable Overlay.
  3. Layout Mode: Choose "Filter: Only Talking". This keeps your screen clean and only shows a name when audio is detected.
CS2 Warning: Counter-Strike 2 "Trusted Mode" often blocks external overlays. If it doesn't appear, you may need to run the game in "Windowed Fullscreen" mode, or rely on a second monitor/phone for status.

7. You are LAN Ready!

You have built the ultimate low-latency communication setup.

  • Bitrate: Maxed out (Crystal clear voice).
  • Latency: ~10ms (Faster than human reaction time).
  • Immersion: Positional Audio enabled.
  • Privacy: 100% Offline/LAN.

Enjoy the session! If you hear an echo, lower the speaker volume or switch to headphones.
